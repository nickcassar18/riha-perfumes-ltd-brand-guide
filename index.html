05 · The rules

In one line.

Use the files, don't retype the logo; orange on charcoal, white on orange, charcoal on light; always spell it Riħa.

Do

  • Use the official logo files from the kit
  • Keep clear space around the logo
  • Spell it Riħa, with the ħ
  • Keep the domain and handles plain: rihaperfumes.com, @rihaperfumesofficial
  • Sound confident, approachable and modern

Don't

  • Recolour, stretch, rotate or add shadows to the logo
  • Type the logo out in a font
  • Put orange on peach or white on light grey
  • Show other brands' logos as if they were Riħa's
  • Write "Riha" in copy where Riħa is meant
